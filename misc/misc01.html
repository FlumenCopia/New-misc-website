About Us

A sound house from Kerala, built for language-first audio.

Misc Audible is the dedicated audible production arm of Misc Archive. We started in 2020 in Kochi with one treated room, a pair of good headphones, and a very stubborn commitment to clean Malayalam dialogue.

4 years and 8000+ billed production hours later, we run podcast seasons, audiobook catalogs, documentary sound and ad mixes — in Malayalam, English, Hindi, Tamil and a handful of dialects in between.

We’re small by choice. 6 engineers, 2 producers, 1 casting director. No account managers. You talk to the person turning the knobs.
